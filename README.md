## 🎧 Subgenre Directory

### 🚗 1. Drift Phonk

- **Tempo:** 120 – 160 BPM
- **Key Musical Elements:** Heavy cowbell melodies, hyper-distorted 808 bass, fast hi-hat patterns, dark Memphis rap vocal loops. High-energy and aggressive.
- **Definitive Tracks:**
  - _DVRST_ – "Close Eyes"
  - _Kordhell_ – "Murder In My Mind"
  - _PLAYAMANE & xander_ – "Midnight"
  * _PHONKHA, g3ox_em_ – "247"
  * _ZXKAI, slxughter_ – "NO BATIDÃO"
  * _Pradaascope_ – "SHINIGAMI"

### 🇧🇷 2. Brazilian Phonk (Funk Mandelão / Montagem Phonk)

- **Tempo:** 125 – 140 BPM
- **Key Musical Elements:** Aggressive Brazilian Baile Funk riddims, heavy metallic percussion ("caixa" snares, syncopated chops), raw industrial distortion, and energetic vocal chants.
- **Definitive Tracks:**
  - _Phonkha & mtg_ – "Subliminal"
  - _S3B4S_ – "MONTAGE - PR FUNK"
  - _Dj FKU_ – "AXEL F FUNK"
  * _Nakama_ – "DIA DELÍCIA"
  * _h6itam_ – "MONTAGEM ALQUIMIA"
  * _DYSMANE_ – "CHIVO FUNK"

### 📼 3. Rare / OG Phonk (Memphis Revival)

- **Tempo:** 85 – 115 BPM
- **Key Musical Elements:** Laid-back cassette-tape warmth, vinyl crackle, sample-heavy jazz and funk chords, muted Roland TR-808 cowbells, heavily pitched cassette rap vocals.
- **Definitive Tracks:**
  - _DJ Smokey_ – "Evil Wayz"
  - _Soudiere_ – "High As Hell"
  - _Devilish Trio_ – "Southern Hospitality"
  * _Roland Jones_ – "Pimpin Ain't Easy"
  * _Freddie Dredd_ – "Cha Cha"
  * _Baker Ya Maker_ – "Born 2 Lose"

### 🌊 4. Wave Phonk

- **Tempo:** 110 – 140 BPM
- **Key Musical Elements:** Atmospheric synth pads, reese basslines, emotional melodies combined with traditional Phonk 808 drums and chopped vocals. Bridges Phonk and Wave/Vaporwave aesthetics.
- **Definitive Tracks:**
  - _Barnacle Boi_ – "Don't Cry"
  - _Skeler_ – "Tel Aviv"
  - _LXST CXNTURY_ – "ODIUM"
  - _Kipish_ – "Horizon"
  - _deadman_ – "Omiya"

### ⚡ 5. Aggressive Phonk / Hardcore Phonk

- **Tempo:** 140 – 170 BPM
- **Key Musical Elements:** Extreme overdrive, clipping distortion, fast double-time percussion, metal-inspired soundscapes, and intense screaming or shouted vocals.
- **Definitive Tracks:**
  - _SCARLXRD_ – "ALWAYS WORKIN"
  - _Ghostemane_ – "Fed Up"
  - _SHADXWBXRN_ – "WARLORD"
  - _Hensonn_ – "Sahara"
  - _Kordhell x Scarlxrd_ – "MISS ME?"

### 🌌 6. Cosmic / Chill Phonk

- **Tempo:** 90 – 120 BPM
- **Key Musical Elements:** Ambient soundscapes, spacey reverb, lush electric piano cords, smooth tempo, and relaxed 808 rhythms. Focuses on vibe rather than aggression.
- **Definitive Tracks:**
  - _Mythic_ – "Space Drift"
  - _LXST CXNTURY_ – "NEVERLAND"
  - _Bored Mami_ – "Starry Night"
  - _V21_ – "Stargazing"

### 🔌 7. Plugg Phonk

- **Tempo:** 130 – 150 BPM
- **Key Musical Elements:** Bright, catchy synthesizer leads (Zaytoven style), minimalist drum patterns, deep sub-bass, and smooth Memphis rap vocal chops.
- **Definitive Tracks:**
  - _Xavier Wulf_ – "Check It Out"
  - _Roland Jones_ – "Pimpin Ain't Easy"
  - _Soudiere x DJ Smokey_ – "Robbery"

## 📖 Glossary of Terms

- **808 Cowbells:** The pitch-shifted cowbell sound originating from the Roland TR-808 drum machine, heavily used to create lead melodies in Drift and Classic Phonk.
- **Memphis Rap Vocals:** Lo-fi vocal chops sampled from 1990s cassette tapes recorded by Memphis hip-hop pioneers (e.g., Three 6 Mafia, Tommy Wright III, Kingpin Skinny Pimp).
- **Chopped & Screwed:** A technique developed by DJ Screw involving slowing down the tempo of a track (between 60–70 BPM) and applying cuts, chops, and pitch-bends.
- **Phonk Acapellas:** Isolated, lo-fi vocal stems extracted from obscure cassette recordings, often treated with pitch manipulation, reverb, and telephone filter effects.
- **Distorted Basslines:** Overdriven, clipped 808 sub-bass notes that saturate the low-end frequencies, giving modern Drift and Brazilian Phonk their wall-of-sound energy.

## 📦 Producer Resources & Sample Packs

### Free & Open Sample Packs

- **Memphis Rap Vaults:** Collection of public domain/archived 1990s Memphis cassette acapellas and vocal snippets.
- **Roland TR-808 Clean & Processed Kits:** Essential drum samples containing original and overdriven 808 cowbells, snares, and kicks.
- **Tape Cassette Emulation Presets:** Open-source Impulse Responses (IR) and FX chains for simulating analog warmth and tape saturation.

### VST Plugins & Production Tools (Free/Freemium)

- **Caelum Audio Tape Cassette 2:** Warm analog tape saturation and flutter plugin.
- **Valhalla Supermassive:** High-end free reverb/delay plugin for Wave and Cosmic Phonk pads.
- **CamelCrusher:** Legacy distortion/compressor plugin widely used for aggressive 808 distortion.

## 🛠️ Open-Source Tools & GitHub Repositories

- [daily-phonk](https://github.com/topics/phonk) – CLI scripts and bots for fetching daily trending Phonk tracks across streaming platforms.
- [premix](https://github.com/topics/audio-processing) – Command-line audio processing tools for automatic BPM detection and pitch correction.
- [Le-Phonk](https://github.com/topics/audio-visualization) – Audio visualizer template for rendering drift video edits with synchronized audio spectrums.
- [spotify-phonk-scraper](https://github.com/topics/spotify-api) – Python script for scraping micro-genres and emerging Phonk artists from Spotify's API.
