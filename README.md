# 🎧 What is Phonk?

Phonk is a broad music style rooted heavily in **1990s Memphis rap**, particularly its dark production, chopped vocals, heavy bass, and lo-fi atmosphere.

During the 2010s, producers on platforms such as SoundCloud helped revive and reinterpret those sounds.

Then the late 2010s and early 2020s brought **Drift Phonk**, which pushed the sound toward faster tempos, distorted 808s, loud cowbells, and electronic production. The style became heavily associated with drifting, cars, anime, gaming, fitness, and short-form video.

Later, Brazilian Funk, Montagem, Wave, EDM, Hardstyle and other regional/internet scenes started crossing over with Phonk.

So today, "Phonk" can mean very different things depending on what part of the scene you're exploring.

```text
1990s Memphis Rap
        ↓
2010s Memphis Revival / Internet Phonk
        ↓
Late-2010s Drift Phonk
        ↓
2020–2022 Viral Phonk
        ↓
2023+ Brazilian Funk / Phonk
        ↓
Montagem + Regional Hybrids
        ↓
Current Internet Phonk Ecosystem
```

# 📅 Phonk Timeline

## 📼 Before 2020 — The Foundation

**Era:** Memphis Revival / Underground Phonk

**Typical Tempo:** ~80–120 BPM

Before the modern viral Phonk era, the genre was much closer to underground hip-hop.

The sound commonly featured:

- Memphis rap samples
- Cassette hiss
- Vinyl noise
- Dark jazz/funk samples
- Heavy 808 bass
- Chopped vocals
- Cowbells
- Pitch manipulation
- Lo-fi mastering
- Chopped & screwed influence

The 2010s Memphis revival was important in establishing the modern internet Phonk scene.

# 🏎️ 2020 — Drift Phonk Takes Shape

**Era:** Early Drift Phonk

**Typical Tempo:** ~120–160 BPM

The modern Drift Phonk sound developed from the older Phonk formula but became much more aggressive.

The combination of:

- distorted 808s
- TR-808 cowbells
- dark melodies
- Memphis vocal samples
- driving drums
- electronic production

created a sound that worked extremely well with car and drift footage.

Drift Phonk had developed in the late 2010s, particularly among producers in Russia and Eastern Europe, before gaining much wider attention through TikTok around 2020.

# 🏁 2021 — Drift Phonk Breaks Out

**Era:** Drift / Car Edit Phonk

**Typical Tempo:** ~120–170 BPM

Drift Phonk became much easier to recognize as its own style.

Its visual culture became closely connected with:

- Japanese cars
- drifting

- anime

# 📱 2022 — The Viral Explosion

**Era:** TikTok / Shorts / Edit Culture

**Typical Tempo:** ~120–180 BPM

2022 pushed Phonk far outside its previous underground audience.

Short-form platforms became a major way for listeners to discover the genre.

Phonk started appearing constantly in:

- TikTok edits
- YouTube Shorts
- Instagram Reels
- Gym edits
- Anime edits
- Gaming clips
- Car edits
- Meme videos

### Important tracks

- _INTERWORLD_ — "METAMORPHOSIS"
- _MoonDeity_ — "NEON BLADE"
- _Kordhell_ — "Murder In My Mind"
- _DVRST_ — "Close Eyes"
- _Hensonn_ — "Sahara"

### One major change

For many new listeners, Phonk no longer meant Memphis rap first.

Instead:

```text
Phonk
 ↓
Cowbells
 ↓
Distorted 808
 ↓
Drift / Gym / Anime Edit
```

This created a growing gap between **underground Phonk** and **viral Drift Phonk**, even though they shared musical roots.

# 🇧🇷 2023 — Brazilian Funk Enters the Global Scene

**Era:** Brazilian Funk / Phonk Crossover

**Typical Tempo:** ~120–160 BPM

Brazilian Funk began becoming much more visible in international internet music culture.

The resulting tracks often combined:

- Brazilian Funk rhythms
- Portuguese vocals
- Heavy bass
- Distortion
- Phonk-style mixing
- Short vocal phrases
- Aggressive drops

The term **Brazilian Phonk** became widely used online, although Brazilian Funk itself has a much longer history and should not simply be treated as a Phonk subgenre.

### Important example

- _S3BZS, MC GW & MC Menor do Alvorada_ — "MONTAGEM - PR FUNK"

Billboard Brasil documented the international growth of this Brazilian Phonk/Funk crossover and its strong streaming performance.

### Related styles

| Style           | Main connection                              |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Brazilian Phonk | Phonk + Brazilian Funk                       |
| Funk Automotivo | Car-oriented Brazilian Funk                  |
| Funk Mandelão   | São Paulo Funk scene                         |
| Montagem        | Edited/chopped Funk structures               |
| MTG             | "Montagem" style associated with edited Funk |

- Funk percussion
- Vocal chops
- Slowed versions
- Sped-up versions
- Edit-friendly arrangements

The boundary between **Phonk, Brazilian Funk, Funk edits and internet electronic music** became increasingly blurry.

# 📖 Glossary

### 808 Cowbell

The distinctive cowbell sound associated with the Roland TR-808 drum machine. It became one of the most recognizable sounds in modern Drift Phonk.

### Memphis Rap Vocals

Vocal material originating from or influenced by the underground Memphis rap scene, particularly recordings from the 1990s.

### Chopped & Screwed

A production technique associated with DJ Screw involving slowing down tracks, chopping sections, repeating phrases and manipulating pitch.

### Phonk Acapellas

Isolated or extracted vocal material used in Phonk production. The original recordings may be copyrighted, so producers should always consider licensing and sample clearance.

### Distorted Basslines

808/sub-bass sounds processed with saturation, clipping or distortion.

### Montagem

A term associated with edited/chopped Brazilian Funk productions. It has become especially common in current international internet music.

### Brazilian Phonk

A broad internet label for tracks combining Phonk-associated production with Brazilian Funk influences.

### Drift Phonk

A faster, electronic-oriented Phonk style associated with distorted bass, cowbells and internet car/drift culture.

### Wave Phonk

An atmospheric branch combining Phonk elements with Wave-inspired synths, bass and reverb-heavy production.

- Which scenes crossed over?
- Which artists were important?
- Which tracks represent the period?

For future years, don't write predictions as facts.

For example, **2026 should remain an ongoing section** until the year is finished.

## 4. Adding Producer Resources

Good submissions include:

- Free plugins
- Open-source tools
- Legitimate sample packs
- Production tutorials
- Audio-processing software
- Public-domain resources
- Producer communities

Please do **not** submit:

- Pirated plugins
- Cracked software
- Copyright-infringing sample dumps
- Malware
- Suspicious download mirrors
- Re-uploaded commercial sample packs
